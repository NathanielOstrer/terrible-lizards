The Terrible Lizards

A pixel nature documentary. 5 minutes.

About

This film follows the dinosaurs from the Late Jurassic to a garden today.

The narrator is a voice made in Breeze TTS 2 from a written description: an elderly English naturalist, soft, warm and slightly breathy. Nathaniel picked it from eleven auditions. Each line has its own direction.

Code makes everything else: the dinosaurs, the habitats, the camera, the music and the sound effects.

Science notes

  • Velociraptor was the size of a turkey, and it had feathers.
  • Pteranodon was a pterosaur. It was not a dinosaur.
  • The crest of Parasaurolophus probably worked as a horn for its calls.
  • Birds are living dinosaurs.
